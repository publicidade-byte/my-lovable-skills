# Support reply

## Tone

- Professional, warm, concise.
- Acknowledge frustration without over-apologizing.
- Second person ("you") — avoid blame.

## Structure

1. **Acknowledge** the issue in one sentence.
2. **Answer / fix** steps numbered if procedural.
3. **Next step** — what you need from them OR what happens next.
4. **Close** — offer follow-up; no guilt trips.

## When info is missing

Ask one focused question; do not bombard with ten questions.

## Escalation

If billing, data loss, or security: note internal escalation path; do not promise refunds not in policy.

## Avoid

- Jargon without explanation.
- "As per my last email" tone.
- Sharing other customers' data.
- Making up refund/credit policy — use placeholders `[confirm with billing policy]`.

## Output format

Ready to paste email/chat; subject line if email.
